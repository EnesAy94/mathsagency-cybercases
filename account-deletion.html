Account Deletion Request

Last updated: June 1, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and associated data for the mobile game Maths Agency: Cyber Cases, developed by METYO Studio.

App Information

  • App Name: Maths Agency: Cyber Cases
  • Developer: METYO Studio
  • Package Name: com.metyostudio.mathsagency

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your account and all associated data, please send an email to:

Email: metyostudiotr@gmail.com
Subject line: Account Deletion Request – Maths Agency

In your email, please include:

  • The Google account email address you used to sign in to the game
  • A brief confirmation that you wish to permanently delete your account and game data

We will process your request and confirm deletion by email within 30 days of receiving your request.

What Data Will Be Deleted

The following data will be permanently deleted from our servers:

  • Your Firebase authentication record (linked to your Google account)
  • Your cloud save data (game progress, scores, achievements, statistics, settings)
  • Your leaderboard entry (player name, score, profile picture ID)
  • Any in-game profile data (nickname, selected cosmetics)

What Data May Be Retained

For legal and accounting compliance, the following data may be retained for the periods required by applicable law:

  • In-app purchase records: Google Play retains purchase receipts independently of our systems. We do not store payment details — these are held only by Google Play Billing and are subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Anonymized crash logs: Firebase Crashlytics may retain anonymized crash diagnostics that are not linked to your personal identity, for product improvement purposes.

Alternative: In-Game Deletion

You may also request account deletion from within the game by signing in with your Google account and contacting us through the support email shown in the Settings menu. The email-based method described above remains the official channel.

Contact

If you have any questions about this process, or did not receive confirmation within 30 days, please contact:

METYO Studio
